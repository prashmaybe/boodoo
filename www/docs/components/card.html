Card

Budu's cards provide a flexible and extensible content container with multiple variants and options.

About

A card is a flexible and extensible content container. It includes options for headers and footers, a wide variety of content, contextual background colors, and powerful display options. If you're familiar with Bootstrap's cards, Budu's will feel right at home.
