boodoo vs Bootstrap

Compare boodoo and Bootstrap to see how they differ in architectural philosophy, customization, bundle footprint, and developer experience.

Why developers choose boodoo over Bootstrap

If you love Bootstrap's zero-config drop-in components but want the utility flexibility of Tailwind and the elevation polish of Material Design, boodoo gives you all three in one lightweight, modern toolkit.
