Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026. This Privacy Policy describes how Pebble Benders ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and handles technical data when you visit boodooframework.com or use the boodoo open-source framework.

1 · Core Privacy Philosophy

boodoo is designed from the ground up with a privacy-first, zero-telemetry architecture:

  • Zero Framework Telemetry: The open-source boodoo library (installed via npm, yarn, pnpm, or CDN) runs entirely within your browser or mobile client. It contains zero analytics beacons, zero tracking pixels, zero background phone-home pings, and zero external runtime dependencies.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, create an account, or share personal credentials to read our documentation, copy code snippets, or download framework bundles and starter kits.
  • No Third-Party Advertising: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal data with third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing profiling companies.
100% Client-Side Zero Telemetry
Independent & Private

When you integrate boodoo into your own projects, your end users' interactions remain 100% private between them and your infrastructure. Boodoo neither monitors nor intercepts any user traffic.

2 · Information Collected on Our Website

When you browse our documentation site at boodooframework.com, we collect only the minimal data necessary to deliver static assets and protect our infrastructure:

  • Standard Web Server Logs: Like nearly all websites, our web hosting infrastructure automatically logs basic technical request metadata: your IP address, browser type and version (User-Agent), operating system, referring URL, requested page URL, and the timestamp of the request. These logs are used solely for diagnosing technical errors, maintaining server stability, preventing distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks, and security auditing.
  • Direct Communications: If you voluntarily contact us via email (e.g. for enterprise support or inquiries), we retain your email address and message contents solely to respond to your request.

3 · Cookies and Local Browser Storage

We do not use tracking cookies or persistent advertising identifiers. We utilize local browser storage (localStorage) exclusively for client-side user experience preferences:

Storage Key Mechanism Purpose Duration
boodoo-theme Local Storage Stores your chosen theme preference (light or dark mode) so the documentation displays your preferred color scheme on subsequent visits. Persistent until cleared
boodoo_acquisition_source Local Storage First-party, client-only attribution that records the initial referral query parameter (e.g., utm_source or referring domain) directly on your device without sending it to third-party ad networks. Persistent until cleared

You can clear or disable local storage at any time through your browser's security settings without impacting your ability to read the documentation.

4 · Third-Party Services & CDNs

To provide fast global delivery of documentation and assets, our site interacts with trusted third-party providers:

  • Content Delivery Networks (CDNs): Downloadable starter packages, release archives, and precompiled CSS/JS distributions are distributed via public CDNs (including jsDelivr, Cloudflare, and GitHub). These providers process technical network requests according to their own privacy policies.
  • Web Fonts: The documentation references Google Fonts (Inter and JetBrains Mono) to render typography. Requests to Google Fonts servers are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • External Links: Our documentation links to external websites and repositories (such as GitHub, npm, and Figma). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party platforms.

5 · Data Retention & Security

We take reasonable technical and administrative precautions to safeguard data:

  • HTTPS Enforcement: All traffic between your browser and boodooframework.com is encrypted using Modern TLS (Transport Layer Security) with Strict-Transport-Security (HSTS) headers.
  • Log Purging: Server access and error logs are automatically rotated and deleted on a rolling 30-day schedule.

6 · Your Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA / CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have statutory rights under privacy regulations such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA):

  • Right of Access & Portability: You have the right to request information regarding any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Erasure & Rectification: You have the right to request deletion or correction of any personal data you have voluntarily provided to us.
  • No Sale of Personal Data: We do not sell, share, or monetize personal information.

Because we do not maintain accounts, subscriber lists, or user profiles on this documentation site, we do not store identifying personal data in the ordinary course of browsing.

7 · Children's Privacy

boodoo is a software development toolkit intended for developers, designers, and engineering organizations. Our website is not directed toward children under 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

8 · Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect framework updates, operational improvements, or legal requirements. When changes are made, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this document.
