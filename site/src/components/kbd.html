Overview

The .kbd component renders realistic 3D keycaps for depicting keyboard combinations, shortcuts, and navigation hints.

Press ⌘ K to open the command palette.
Save document: Ctrl S
Undo / Redo: Ctrl Z and Ctrl Shift Z
