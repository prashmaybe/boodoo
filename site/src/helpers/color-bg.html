Subtle backgrounds

Use .bg-{color}-subtle for light, tinted backgrounds.

.bg-primary-subtle .bg-success-subtle .bg-danger-subtle .bg-warning-subtle .bg-info-subtle 
<span class="bg-primary-subtle p-3 rounded">.bg-primary-subtle</span>
<span class="bg-success-subtle p-3 rounded">.bg-success-subtle</span>
<span class="bg-danger-subtle p-3 rounded">.bg-danger-subtle</span>
<span class="bg-warning-subtle p-3 rounded">.bg-warning-subtle</span>
<span class="bg-info-subtle p-3 rounded">.bg-info-subtle</span>

Subtle borders

Use .border-{color}-subtle for tinted borders.

.border-success-subtle
