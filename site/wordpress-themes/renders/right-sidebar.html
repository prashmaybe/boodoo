Classic Blog Architecture with Right Sidebar

Published on August 24, 2026 • By Prashant • 4 Comments

The Right Sidebar Theme represents the classic WordPress content layout. Primary article streams occupy 66% of the screen width, while category widgets, search bars, and author callouts reside in the 33% right sidebar.

Whether publishing daily news updates, technical tutorials, or personal blog posts, the right sidebar provides easy access to related links without obstructing the main reading experience.
